# Paint order — Bearcat Claw (1971 Chevrolet Camaro Z28)

Sprayed on the INSIDE of the clear body. Paint order below is sprayed in this exact sequence — index 0 goes on first and ends up the colour you SEE from outside once the body is mounted.

_Claw slashes read as motion at speed; BEARCAT sits above the rear arch where curvature is lowest; a single chevron on the hood centreline keeps the front clean._

Step 1 — Prep: wash the inside with dish soap, dry fully, tack cloth. Do not touch the painting surface after this.
Step 2 — Apply layer RED (mask-c2-sheet1.svg). Align the registration crosses to the panel edge — do NOT remove any previously applied mask.
Step 3 — Spray Tamiya PS-34. 3 light coats, ~10 minutes between coats.
Step 4 — Apply layer BLACK (mask-c1-sheet1.svg). Align the registration crosses to the panel edge — do NOT remove any previously applied mask.
Step 5 — Spray Tamiya PS-5. 3 light coats, ~10 minutes between coats.
Step 6 — Remove ALL masks (Red, Black). Every mask applied above stays on until this single step — this is the only time you remove anything before the backing coat.
Final — Backing coat: spray Tamiya PS-1 over the entire inside, no mask. This seals and backs every colour sprayed above.
